# Hatchet Hog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Hatchet Hog is published by Simian Spark. Hatchet Hog does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, account creation, in-app purchases, or social features.

Hatchet Hog stores game progress, personal best scores, settings, and recent run summaries locally on the user's device. This data does not leave the device and can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive permissions.

For privacy questions, contact Simian Spark at JaredBStewart@gmail.com.
